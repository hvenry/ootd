# Wear log

A calendar. Tap a day, then tap the garments worn. One `wear_log` row per day.

- Tagging today replaces the OOTD suggestion with what was actually worn.
- An optional one-tap comfort vote: `too cold`, `right` or `too warm`.
- Later, a generated preview of the day's outfit.

## Analytics

- Most and least worn, and days since last worn.
- Overused combinations, flagged.
- Cost per wear, once prices are recorded.
- The colour spread of what is worn against what is owned.

The same rows train the colour score and the daily model.
