# Roadmap

Build order. One line per spec; reorder lines, never rename files.

1. [Test suite](test-suite.md) - Vitest for units and the homography, before more arithmetic lands on top.
2. [Standardise bake-off](standardize-bakeoff.md) - pick the image model and confirm the mannequin.
3. [Standardise](standardize.md) - every face on one frozen plate, the polished grid and the raw view.
4. [Shoe and belt measuring](shoe-belt-measure.md) - templates and the pin line.
5. [Custom mannequin](custom-mannequin.md) - body readings, a v2 plate, the closet regenerated.
6. [Fit](fit.md) - ease per point and the fit line.
7. [Layering](layering.md) - does the outer layer clear the inner one.
8. [Outfits](outfits.md) - garments worn together on the plate, and the ideas gallery.
9. [Daily outfit](daily-outfit.md) - today's outfit from the forecast, on the home screen.
10. [Wear log](wear-log.md) - the calendar that closes the loop.
11. [Colour](colour.md) - OKLab extraction and the colour score.
